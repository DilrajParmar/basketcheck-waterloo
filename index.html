How it works

  1. Fill your cart. Search or browse the aisles and add what you actually buy.
  2. Set your starting point. Type your address, pick campus, a residence or Uptown, or click the map.
  3. Compare the stores. Each location gets an itemized receipt, a total with HST and a trip time.

Stores we compare

Prices come from each chain's own website. Walk, bike and car times follow real street routes from OpenStreetMap (or an estimate if that service can't be reached); bus and ION times are estimates. Grocers that don't publish prices online appear on the map only.

        Using the site

        How do I compare stores?

        Go to Shop, add the groceries you usually buy, then open Compare stores. Every store with online prices is ranked for your exact cart, with an itemized receipt and total for each one.

        How do I find or add an item?

        Search at the top of the Shop page or pick an aisle. You can search by name (e.g. “Doritos”) or by a general word like “breakfast”, “chips”, “vegan” or “quick meal” to see everything that fits, or tap one of the Popular buttons. Press Add on a product, then use + and − or type an amount to change the quantity. Your cart stays while the page is open, even if you refresh. Each time you open the site it starts with an empty cart, so save any cart you want to keep.

        Can I save my cart?

        Yes. Your current cart resets when you close the page, so save any cart you want to keep, like a weekly shop or a party shop. Type a name under Saved carts on the Compare stores page and press Save cart. Press Load to bring one back. Saved carts live in this browser only, so they won't appear on another device.

        Can I type an exact amount?

        Yes. Click the number in the cart or on a product card and type any amount, then press Enter. Items sold by weight take kilograms with up to two decimals (for example 0.35 kg), and other items take whole numbers. Typing 0 removes the item.

        How do I set where I'm starting from?

        On the Compare stores page, start typing your address and pick it from the suggestions (click one, or use the arrow keys and press Enter), choose a spot from the list, or click anywhere on the map to drop a pin. Press Apply changes to recalculate distances, or Cancel to keep your old spot.

        How do I get directions to a store?

        Open a store's receipt and press the directions button. Google Maps opens with the route from your starting point, using the travel option you picked (walk, bike, bus or car). Show store on Google Maps shows the store on a Google map right on the page.

        Is my address saved or shared?

        It isn't saved. Your starting point is forgotten when you close the page, and nothing is stored on a server. To find your address, what you type is sent to Esri's address search (or Photon, a free OpenStreetMap search, if Esri doesn't answer), and the directions button sends your starting point to Google Maps. See the Privacy page for the full list.

        What do Walk, Bike, Bus / ION and Car change?

        They change the trip time shown for each store and the order under Shortest trip and Recommended. Prices stay the same.

        What does "Recommended" mean?

        It weighs basket cost against the round-trip time, based on your answer to What matters more to you? With Saving money, a farther store wins if it's noticeably cheaper. With Saving time, the closer store wins unless the price gap is large. Both sits in between. Use Lowest price or Shortest trip if you only care about one of them.

        What are meal starters?

        On the Shop page, a Meal starters button shows the groceries for a simple meal, like tacos or a stir fry. Choose how many people you're cooking for and the amounts change to match, with a rough idea of each person's portion. Nothing goes in your cart until you press Add all to cart. Items you already have enough of are left as they are, and you can change amounts or remove items afterwards.

        What is the unit price?

        It's the price per 100 g, per 100 ml or per item (like per roll or per egg), worked out from the pack size and the lowest price we found. It makes different sizes easy to compare. Pick Lowest unit price in the Shop page's Sort menu to see the best value first. Items with a size range, like "12 to 18 pops", have no unit price.

        How does the budget work?

        Type what you want to spend under Budget on the Compare stores page. You'll see the lowest total for your whole cart and how far under or over your budget it is. Cheaper swaps lists similar items in the same aisle and size range that cost less at that store, plus items where picking "Cheapest available" in the Brand menu saves money. Press Swap to change your cart. Swaps are ideas, so check they suit you.

        What does "Split your cart" mean?

        Sometimes buying part of your cart at one store and the rest at another store nearby costs noticeably less. When two stores within 2 km of each other save you at least $2 together, and the two-stop trip is no more than 30 minutes longer than going to one store, a yellow box above the receipts shows which items to buy where, the total, and how long the two-stop trip takes. It never changes the store ranking below it.

        How do I share a cart with roommates?

        On the Compare stores page, press Copy link under Share with roommates and send it. When someone opens it, they're asked before it replaces their own cart. Pick how many people are splitting the bill to see each person's share of the lowest total.

        Prices and stores

        Do any of these stores have a student discount?

        Not that we could confirm for Waterloo Region (checked October 28, 2026). Food Basics gives post-secondary students 10% off on Tuesdays at select stores, but none of the stores it lists are in Waterloo Region. Sobeys' Tuesday student discount is reported only at select Atlantic Canada stores. Bulk Barn has a student discount on Wednesdays for regular-priced items with a student ID. What does work at the stores we compare: PC Optimum points at No Frills, Zehrs, Your Independent Grocer and Real Canadian Superstore, and Scene+ points at Sobeys. Always ask at the till, since store offers change.

        Where do the prices come from?

        From each chain's own website, for its Waterloo Region stores, on the date shown at the top of the page. Sale prices are marked. Prices change often, so check the store before you go.

        Do the totals include tax?

        Yes. Each total includes 13% HST on taxable items, which are marked H on the receipt. Basic groceries like produce, dairy, meat, bread and pantry staples aren't taxed in Ontario, while chips, pop and household items like paper towel and detergent are. Period products such as pads and tampons are HST-free.

        Why does a store say an item is "not available"?

        That store's website didn't list the same product at the same size. The receipt lists every missing item. The store is still ranked using an estimate for those items so the comparison stays fair, and it's flagged so you know.

        Why are some stores only on the map?

        Stores like Food Basics, FreshCo, Farm Boy, Costco and T&T don't post readable prices online, so they can't be compared. They're shown on the map as hollow grey dots with no number, so you still know they're nearby.

        Are the prices for the same brand at every store?

        Yes, by default. Each item is compared as the same product and size at every store, and per-kg items like produce and meat are compared per kilogram. If you pick Cheapest available in an item's Brand menu, each store uses its lowest-priced brand of that item instead, still at the same size.

        How accurate are trip times and store locations?

        Trip times are estimates from distance and typical travel speeds, not live traffic or bus schedules. Pins marked "approximate" may be a few hundred metres off.

        BasketCheck is a free student project that compares grocery prices in Waterloo Region. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't run a server that stores anything about you.

        What other services see

        • Esri address search gets the text you type in the address box, and Photon (komoot) gets it only if Esri doesn't answer. They send back matching addresses.
        • Esri map tiles are loaded for the street map, so Esri sees which part of the map is on screen.
        • Share links contain your cart's item codes, amounts and brand picks in the link itself. Only the people you send a link to can see it.
        • Google Maps gets your starting point and the store only when you press a directions button or "Show store on Google Maps". The embedded Google map may set Google's own cookies.
        • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare) send the font and the map code, so they see a normal web request from your browser.

        Each of these services handles that data under its own privacy policy: Esri, komoot (Photon), Google and Cloudflare.

        Cookies

        BasketCheck doesn't use cookies or tracking, so there's no cookie banner. Browser storage is only used for the cart features listed above.

        Children

        The site is meant for adults and older students doing their own shopping. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
